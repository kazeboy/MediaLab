# Code of Conduct

MediaLab is a small, friendly project. We want everyone who reports a bug, asks a question or sends a pull request to feel welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intentions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code and ideas, not about people.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, threats or intimidation.
- Publishing someone's private information (including cookies, account details or addresses) without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or encouraging any of the above.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, Discussions, and any other channel run for MediaLab) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
